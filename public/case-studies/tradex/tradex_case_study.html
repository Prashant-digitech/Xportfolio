04 · THE CHALLENGE

Reduce the cognitive load.

04 / 12

The design challenge was not simply to show more market information. It was to organize a high-density trading environment so that the most important context remains visible while deeper analysis stays one interaction away.

01 · MARKET DISCOVERY
02 · WATCHLIST
03 · ANALYSIS
04 · PORTFOLIO
05 · ACTION
Challenge 01

Information density

Charts, symbols, indicators, positions and news can compete for attention. The interface needs a clear visual hierarchy.

Challenge 02

Context switching

Moving between market discovery, a specific asset, portfolio analysis and an AI assistant can fragment the task.

Challenge 03

Trust in AI assistance

AI guidance must be presented as an analytical aid, not as a guaranteed prediction or financial recommendation.

DESIGN OPPORTUNITY

Create a single workspace where market context, analysis and portfolio state can be understood without losing the user's place.

05 · RESEARCH & DISCOVERY

Start with what the product must do.

05 / 12

Evidence used in this case study

Documented product architecture and implementation notes.
Existing TradeX UI / UX case-study artifacts and wireframes.
Defined terminal features: watchlist, portfolio, analytics and AI-assisted analysis.
Technical stack and interaction requirements from the project.
Evidence rule: no invented participant counts, survey percentages, testimonials or business outcomes are presented as research findings.

Discovery questions

What comes first?

Market discovery → watchlist → asset analysis.

What stays visible?

Selected asset, key chart context and actionable controls.

Where does AI help?

Summarization, signal exploration and contextual assistance.

How does state persist?

Watchlists, portfolio state and user session are treated as product state.

Product principle

Progressively disclose complexity: give the user a strong overview, then allow deeper analysis when it becomes relevant.

07 · PROBLEM DEFINITION

Turn a terminal into a decision workspace.

07 / 12
PROBLEM STATEMENTS
Too much context, too little hierarchy.
High-density trading information needs a stronger visual order.
Analysis is separated from action.
The transition from chart interpretation to paper-trading should feel deliberate.
AI needs context, not just a chat box.
The assistant should understand the selected asset and surface relevant analysis.
DESIGN GOAL

Make the next useful action obvious without hiding the depth that advanced users need.
